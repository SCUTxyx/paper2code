## Problem formalization

An action chunk $A \in \mathbb{R}^{T_p \times d_a}$ is generated by reversing a diffusion
process from noise, conditioned on observations; the robot executes the first $T_a$ actions
then replans (receding horizon).

## Symbol table

| Symbol | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| $A_t$ (x_t below) | noisy action chunk at diffusion step $t$ | §3 |
| $\beta_t, \bar\alpha_t$ | noise schedule and its cumulative product | §3 (as DDPM, Ho et al. 2006) |
| $\epsilon, \hat\epsilon_\theta$ | true noise / network prediction | §3 Eq.(5) |
| $T_p, T_a$ | prediction / execution horizon | §"receding horizon control" |

## Core formulas

- Training objective (§3 Eq.5, as DDPM):$L = \mathrm{mean}\big(\epsilon - \hat\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t)\big)^2$ with
  $x_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon$ (Ho et al. Eq.4).
- DDIM inference (Song et al., arXiv:2010.02502 Eq.12, η=0; DP's real-world path):
  $x_{\tau-1} = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_{\tau-1}}\,\frac{x_\tau - \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_\tau}\,\hat\epsilon}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_\tau}} + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_{\tau-1}}\,\hat\epsilon$
- Receding horizon (§"receding horizon control"): replan every $T_a$ steps, execute
  $A[0{:}T_a]$; the final chunk runs to $\min(T_p, H - t)$ at episode end; warm-starting
  is optional and not modeled here.

## Algorithm box (inference, transcription)

1. Sample $x_T \sim \mathcal N(0, I)$ (pure-noise start; the closed-form trajectory below
   is the *training-process* view used for verification);
2. For $\tau = T \dots 1$: $\hat\epsilon \leftarrow$ network; apply the DDIM update above;
3. Return $x_0$; execute $x_0[0{:}T_a]$; replan.

## Claims list

- **P-1** (§3, forward process as DDPM): closed-form marginal and iterative noising are the
  same distribution; under the same noise sequence the unrolled weighted sum equals the
  recursion **exactly** (re-derived in this card, mirrors calibration/04_ddpm).
- **P-2** (DDIM, Song et al. Eq.12): with a perfect predictor $\hat\epsilon = \epsilon$, the
  DDIM chain maps the closed-form trajectory onto itself — starting from
  $x_T = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_T}x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_T}\epsilon$ it lands **exactly** on $x_0$
  at $\tau = 0$; with biased $\hat\epsilon$ the endpoint deviates (negative property).
- **P-3** (receding horizon): the executed sequence is the stitched first-$T_a$ rows of each
  replan's chunk, and the final chunk runs to $\min(T_p, H-t)$; actions of a chunk beyond its
  executed window never affect execution (window property).
- **P-4**: training-loss gradient $\partial L/\partial\hat\epsilon = 2(\hat\epsilon-\epsilon)/N$;
  forward reparameterization gradient $\partial x_t/\partial x_0 = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}I$.
